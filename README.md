## What is a market research API?

A market research API is a tool you call to pull facts about markets, rivals and industries. Things like competitor pages, pricing pages, reviews, news and company lists. You give it a search or URL. It gives you clean rows you can save as CSV or JSON.

People also call this a competitive intelligence API or a market data scraper. Same idea: get market and competitor info without copying it by hand.

---

## What can you build?

These **52,455 ready-to-use market research APIs** help you pull competitive intel into your own apps. You can:

- Collect company lists for market sizing
